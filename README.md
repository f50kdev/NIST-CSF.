## 📘 Project Overview

**Incident Report Analysis** is a cybersecurity project created by **Faustino Henriques** during his studies in the **Google Cybersecurity Professional Certificate** program.  

This report demonstrates how to investigate, contain, and mitigate a **phishing-based credential theft** using the **NIST Cybersecurity Framework** (Identify → Protect → Detect → Respond → Recover).

---

## 🕵️‍♂️ Incident Summary

An intern reported being locked out of her internal network account. However, access logs showed active sessions using her credentials — evidence of **unauthorized access**.  

Investigation revealed that the intern received a **phishing email** directing her to a fake login page, where her credentials were stolen. The attacker used them to access and manipulate customer data in the company’s database, resulting in **data exposure and modification**.

---

## 🧩 NIST Framework Breakdown

### 🪪 **Identify**
- Audited all systems, user accounts, and policies.  
- Discovered compromised intern credentials used by a malicious actor.  
- Confirmed partial deletion and alteration of customer records.  

### 🔐 **Protect**
- Enforced **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)**.  
- Limited login attempts to **3 tries**.  
- Trained all employees on **phishing awareness** and credential hygiene.  
- Deployed new **firewall configuration** and **Intrusion Prevention System (IPS)**.  

### 🧠 **Detect**
- Implemented **Intrusion Detection System (IDS)** and **firewall logging** to monitor unauthorized access patterns.  

### 🚨 **Respond**
- Disabled compromised accounts and contained the breach.  
- Performed forensic investigation on affected systems.  
- Informed leadership and prepared customer notification procedures.  

### 🔁 **Recover**
- Restored affected databases from **secure backups**.  
- Verified data integrity post-restoration.  
- Updated internal security policies and reinforced staff training.

## 💡 Reflections & Learning Outcomes

- Practical application of **NIST CSF** in real-world incident management.
